# Sourdough Focaccia

A pillowy, olive-oil rich focaccia with a crisp base and rosemary and sea salt on top. Adapted from Grant Bakes' video and companion recipe.

## Ingredients

### Feed the starter
- 25g leftover sourdough starter
- 50g bread flour
- 50g water

### Dough
- 450g bread flour
- 325g water
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 25g extra-virgin olive oil
- 10g salt

### Pan and topping
- 25g extra-virgin olive oil, for the pan
- 25g extra-virgin olive oil, for dimpling
- 5g salt (about 1 tsp)
- 1g dried rosemary (about 2 tsp)

## Steps

1. **Feed the starter.** Mix the leftover starter with 50g bread flour and 50g water. Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for 6–8 hours, until doubled and bubbly.
2. **Mix the dough.** Stir the active starter and salt into the water until the starter breaks up. Add the flour and mix until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
3. **Work in the olive oil.** Add 25g olive oil and squeeze it through the dough with your hands until absorbed and the dough gathers together.
4. **Stretch and fold.** For the first set, stretch and fold around the edges of the dough 10–20 times, until it starts to resist. Cover and rest for 30 minutes. Repeat a gentler set, rest another 30 minutes, then do one final set. Turn the dough smooth-side up.
5. **Bulk ferment overnight.** Cover the bowl and leave the dough at room temperature for 6–8 hours, or until doubled. A cooler kitchen may take longer.
6. **Shape.** Oil a 9 × 13 in non-stick metal pan with 25g olive oil. Transfer the dough to the pan, turn it to coat both sides, then leave it smooth-side up. Gently stretch it into a rectangle; it can finish filling the pan during the next rise.
7. **Final proof.** Cover and proof for 3–5 hours, until puffy and spread to the edges of the pan. It may need 6–8 hours depending on room temperature. If you are not ready to bake, refrigerate the proofed dough for up to 12 hours.
8. **Dimple and top.** Heat the oven to 232°C (450°F) about 30 minutes before baking. Pour the remaining 25g olive oil over the dough. Oil your fingertips and gently press dimples down to the pan without tearing the dough. Scatter the salt and dried rosemary over the top.
9. **Bake.** Bake on the middle rack for 30–35 minutes, until deeply golden.
10. **Cool.** Lift the focaccia out of the pan onto a wire rack. Let it cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing.

## Additional tips

- Rise times are guides; follow the dough. It should double during the first rise and look puffy and fill the pan after the final proof.
- Use a generous, even coating of olive oil in the pan for a crisp, golden base.
- After the final proof, the covered pan can wait in the fridge for up to 12 hours before topping and baking.
- For other toppings, try olives, sun-dried tomatoes, or fresh herbs.
